Values in Action – our Q3 winners and nominees

Eighteen nominations came in this quarter, from stores, the warehouse, web fulfilment and support office. Our Leadership Team has chosen three winners, who each receive a £75 Grateful for Your Greatness voucher. Congratulations to all three, and thank you to everyone who took the time to nominate a team member.

This quarter's winners

Work Hard

Francesco Vallecoccia

SEO, E-commerce

For teaching himself AI and automation and sharing what he learned, so the SEO and Content teams can spend more time on the work that matters.

Play Hard

Jane McIntyre

131 WOK

For keeping the energy and laughter going through a short-staffed sale launch day – just four months into her time at OB.

Be Kind

Christina Brand

Customer Services

For recognising when customers need extra care, and leading with heart for a grieving customer and a children's charity.
