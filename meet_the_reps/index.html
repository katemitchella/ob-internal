Meet your Listening Forum reps

What are Listening Forums?

Listening Forums are how your voice reaches the people who make decisions at OB – beyond your manager and the People team.

Every quarter, reps from stores, support office, and web and warehouse meet with Internal Comms and Engagement. They bring the questions, ideas and concerns on their team's mind, and every item raised gets a response. Then reps share back what happened – what's changing, and why if something can't move yet.

It's already made a difference: acoustic insulation in meeting rooms, alternative milks in the support office kitchen, the warehouse pool table back, and clearer night shift responsibilities.

The forums only work because of the people in this course. So here they are.
